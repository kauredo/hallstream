Hallstream for OBS

The program scores basketball, futsal, handball, volleyball and soccer.
It reads the scoreboard console through the SL Stream Box, over
Bluetooth, a Daktronics or Bodet console over a cable, or takes the
score from a phone. It keeps the squads and sponsors of each age
group, and shows the graphics in OBS in a single browser source.

The phone scorer works for all five sports. The console has only been
tested on basketball. Futsal, handball, volleyball and soccer on the
console are untested until someone records a real game. If you try it,
please tell us how it went. The FPB import is for Portuguese basketball
only.

There are three versions. Download the one for your computer:
- Windows 10 or newer, with Bluetooth: the .zip file. Use
  "Hallstream.exe". The "Hallstream (32-bit).exe" is only for 32-bit
  Windows.
- A Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or newer, with an Intel or Apple chip:
  the -macos.dmg file.
- 64-bit Linux, with Bluetooth through BlueZ: the -linux-x64.tar.gz
  file.

The steps below are for Windows. On a Mac or on Linux, also read "ON A
MAC AND ON LINUX", further down.

The panel opens in a Hallstream window. A red icon with a shield also
stays next to the Windows clock. If you can't see it, press the arrow ^
beside the icons.
- Closing the window doesn't close Hallstream: the overlay and the
  stream carry on. Press the icon to see the panel again.
- Right-click it for "Copy overlay address", the box status and "Quit",
  which closes the program.
- Opening the program when it is already open just shows the panel.
- Links in the panel, such as the YouTube sign-in and the GitHub form,
  open in the browser.
- On a PC without Microsoft's WebView2 (it comes with Windows 11 and an
  up-to-date Windows 10), and in "Hallstream (32-bit).exe", the panel
  opens in an Edge window.
- The Help tab in the panel has the steps for game day and what to do
  when something goes wrong.
- The program also writes every value to text files
  (dados\score\*.txt), for anyone who uses them in OBS.


FIRST TIME (AT HOME)

1. Unzip everything to C:\Hallstream and don't move the folder. The
   program keeps everything in the "dados" folder inside it. To make a
   backup, press "Save backup" in Settings (see "BACKUP AND ANOTHER
   COMPUTER").
2. Open "Hallstream.exe". The panel opens by itself.
   - If "Windows protected your PC" appears, press "More info" and then
     "Run anyway".
   - The panel asks if you want a desktop shortcut. Press "Create".
     There is also a button in Settings.
3. Install OBS (version 31 or newer) and DroidCam on the PC and on the
   tablet, and close OBS.
4. In the Screens tab you will see "First-time setup is missing". Press
   "Start". There are seven steps, one at a time: language, club,
   scoring (the console or a phone), OBS ("Set up OBS"), YouTube,
   YouTube channel and a rehearsal. You can stop halfway, as the panel
   remembers where you were. It is also in Settings > First-time setup.
   - At the YouTube step, if only the "Connect to YouTube" button shows,
     just press it. If the step asks for the OAuth client ID and secret,
     bring them, made beforehand with the guide YouTube\CONNECT-YOUTUBE.txt
     (see "LIVE STREAMS ON YOUTUBE"). Without them, press "Skip for now" and
     connect the YouTube account in OBS itself, in Settings > Stream.
   - For the PC to open everything by itself, turn on "Open with
     Windows" in Settings > On this computer.
   - New camera? Choose it in Settings > Cameras and press "Save and
     set up OBS". The old one is hidden in OBS.
   - In Settings > Club, type the name of the home side, the short code
     and the "Club name on the FPB", exactly as it appears in the FPB
     calendar. With that name, the program creates the club's age groups
     and fetches the players and photos from the FPB. If that doesn't
     happen by itself, press "Import club teams" in the Squad tab. Check
     them and add what is missing, such as the coaches.
5. Sponsors tab: upload the logos for each age group and mark the main
   one, if there is one. Those under "General" appear in every age
   group. The screens' strip shows them in this order.
6. To practise without the box: Settings > Demo mode.
7. The scoreboard sits in the top left corner. To move it to the bottom:
   Settings > Graphics > Scoreboard position.


ON GAME DAY

Before you start
1. Put the tablet on the tripod, plugged in and facing the court, and
   open DroidCam.
2. Close the SL Live Stream app on all phones and tablets. The box only
   accepts one connection at a time.
3. Switch on the scoreboard console and the PC. Hallstream opens by
   itself (or from the shortcut) and opens OBS.
4. In the Screens tab, wait for the box to say "Connected" and for OBS
   to say "Ready", in green. If "Repair" appears, press it. If the box
   won't connect, see the Scoring tab.
5. Game tab: choose the age group and the FPB game. The opponent, the
   crest, the time and the hall fill in by themselves. Mark who is
   playing today.
   - The points for each quarter of the last game are only cleared when
     you choose another game, another age group or another date. For a
     second manual game on the same day, or after testing the console,
     press "New game", at the bottom of the Game tab.
6. Only if YouTube is not connected in Hallstream: in the Screens tab,
   press "YouTube thumbnail" and then "Download". In YouTube Studio, set
   the image as the thumbnail of the live video.
7. Screens tab: when it says "Ready to stream", press "Start stream". If
   something is missing, the list below says what. The live stream only
   starts when you press it. With YouTube connected, Hallstream creates
   the live stream with the game's title and the thumbnail, and shows
   the link.
   To try it out first, "Test stream" makes a private live stream: only
   the channel's account can see it, in YouTube Studio. It stays private
   at the end.

During the stream (Screens tab)
8.  Before the game: "Opening", then "Squad" to introduce the team, and
    "Scoreboard" when the ball goes up.
9.  Leave "Automatic" on. The break and the final score appear by
    themselves.
10. In a timeout, press "Timeout" and, when the game restarts,
    "Scoreboard".
11. To show a player, press their name.
12. "Supporters" shows the sponsors large, for example before the game
    or during the break.
13. In a technical pause, press "Back soon" and, when everything is
    back, "Scoreboard". To put a line of text underneath, type it in
    "Back soon message" and press "Save message".
14. In the break or at the end, "Next game" shows the age group's next
    game. The FPB only gives the games for the next 14 days. If there is
    no game in that period, the button turns grey and says why.

With the Scoreboard on air, a sponsor appears for 10 seconds in the top
right corner, every 4 minutes, in the order of the strip. To turn it off
or change the minutes, go to Settings > Graphics.

At the end
15. "Save score image" makes the image for Instagram.
16. In Screens, press "End stream" and confirm. With YouTube connected,
    a minute or two later the video becomes unlisted and the link
    appears, with "Copy" and "Share on WhatsApp", to send to the
    players.
17. To close the program, right-click the icon next to the clock and
    choose "Quit". If the stream is still on air, the program asks
    before closing.


ON A MAC AND ON LINUX

The rest of this guide still applies, with these differences:
- The data is not in C:\Hallstream\dados (see below for where it is).
  Where the guide says dados\, read dados/.
- In "Set up OBS" and in Settings > Cameras you can't choose a USB
  camera or a capture card yet. Use a phone or a tablet, with DroidCam
  or through VDO.Ninja.
- OBS opens with its window showing. Leave it open.
- The panel doesn't offer a desktop shortcut.

On a Mac
1. Open the .dmg file and drag Hallstream into the Applications folder.
   Always open it from there. Opened straight from the .dmg, "Open when
   I sign in to the Mac" doesn't work.
2. The first time, the Mac asks whether you want to open an app
   downloaded from the internet. Press "Open".
3. When the Mac asks about Bluetooth, press "Allow". If you refused, go
   to System Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth and turn
   Hallstream on.
- On a Mac the panel opens in a Hallstream window, not the browser. The
  panel still opens in the browser on phones and other computers, as
  before.
- Closing the window doesn't close Hallstream: the overlay and the
  stream carry on. Press the Dock icon, or open Hallstream again, to see
  the window.
- To close Hallstream, use Hallstream > Quit Hallstream (⌘Q), "Quit" in
  the menu bar icon's menu, or Quit in the Dock icon's menu. If the stream is
  on air, the program asks before closing.
- Hallstream also has an icon in the menu bar, at the top right: "Open
  panel", "Copy overlay address", the box status and "Quit".
- Links out of the panel, such as the help and the YouTube sign-in,
  open in the browser. View > Open the Panel in the Browser opens the
  panel there, if you prefer.
- Instead of "Open with Windows", Settings > On this computer has "Open
  when I sign in to the Mac".
- The data is in ~/Library/Application Support/Hallstream/dados.
  Settings shows the path in the Demo card. In Finder, use Go > Go to
  Folder and paste the path.
- To use a backup, close Hallstream and unzip it into
  ~/Library/Application Support/Hallstream.

On Linux
1. Extract the .tar.gz file where you will keep it, for example in your
   home folder. You get a Hallstream folder. Don't move it: the data is
   kept inside it, in Hallstream/dados.
2. Open a terminal in the Hallstream folder and type ./Hallstream. The
   panel opens by itself in the browser.
- The icon sits in the notification area. Press it to open the panel;
  right-click it for the menu.
- The icon needs a desktop that shows AppIndicator (StatusNotifier)
  icons. Ubuntu already has one. On other GNOME desktops, install the
  "AppIndicator and KStatusNotifierItem Support" extension. With no
  icon, open the panel at http://127.0.0.1:8765/painel and close the
  program with Ctrl+C in the terminal.
- Bluetooth goes through BlueZ. On some systems your user has to be in
  the bluetooth group. "Open Bluetooth settings" doesn't work here: open
  them by hand.
- Install zenity. Without it, the program writes its messages only to
  the terminal and asks nothing before closing, even with the stream on
  air.
- Install wl-clipboard or xclip so that "Copy overlay address" works.
- The program opens the system's OBS or the Flathub OBS. "Close OBS on
  quit" doesn't exist on Linux yet: at the end, close OBS by hand.
- Instead of "Open with Windows", Settings > On this computer has "Open
  when I sign in".
- To use a backup, close Hallstream and unzip it inside the Hallstream
  folder.


CLUB VIDEOS (PROMO)

In the Promo tab, upload MP4 videos up to 200 MB, for example the
shop's advert filmed on a phone.
- For each video, type the label, the title and a line of text. You can
  add a link with a QR code.
- Choose whether it has sound, the volume and what happens at the end:
  go back to the previous screen or repeat.
- "Show promo" puts the video on air full screen. "Stop and go back"
  takes it off.
- A vertical video can also play small beside the camera during a
  timeout ("Show small during timeout").
The videos are kept in the folder dados\promo.


SQUADS ALWAYS UP TO DATE

Once a day, and when you choose the game, the program goes to the FPB
to fetch the new players, the numbers and the photos for each age
group.
- It deletes nobody, and what you typed by hand stays as it is.
- The Squad tab shows when it last ran and what changed.
- In a new season, the FPB creates new teams and the program moves each
  age group to the right team. Anyone no longer on the team is taken
  off the games and goes under "No longer in the team". If the program
  doesn't know which team is the new one, the Squad tab asks you to
  choose it.


IF THE PROGRAM CAN'T FIND THE BOX

Open the Scoring tab in the panel.
- If "Bluetooth off" appears, press "Open Bluetooth settings" and turn
  it on.
- The list shows the Bluetooth devices nearby, strongest signal first.
  The box is usually marked "Likely". If it isn't, put the PC next to
  the box and try the ones with the strongest signal.
- Press "Try". If it is the box, the program connects and remembers it
  for next time. If it isn't, it says so and you can try another.
- If nothing works, send the file dados\bluetooth.log, which keeps
  everything the program saw.


A CONSOLE ON A CABLE (DAKTRONICS OR BODET)

The program also reads two consoles connected to the PC by a serial
cable (RS-232), with no Stream Box:
- Daktronics All Sport 5000, for basketball and volleyball. Connect the
  J6 "MAIN PORT" (25 pins, on the back of the console) to the PC with a
  straight 9-to-25-pin serial cable and a USB-to-serial adapter. The data
  comes out as RS-232 on pin 3, with ground on pin 7. Pins 22 and 7 carry
  the same data as a current loop, which needs a converter.
- Bodet Scorepad, for basketball and handball. The protocol output comes
  out of the main keyboard's RJ45 socket. Bodet's manual connects it to
  an RJ45-to-RS232 interface (a Moxa NPort), and that interface to the PC.
Neither has been tested with a real console: the codes come from the
manuals. If you have one, record a game and send us the file.

1. Scoring tab: choose "The console, by cable".
2. Choose the console and the port. If you don't know which port it is,
   unplug the cable, press "Search again" and see which port disappears.
3. The line underneath says what is happening. "Reading the console"
   shows the score that arrives, so you can compare it with the hall's
   scoreboard.
- On a Daktronics, press STOP on the console once after connecting: it
  only sends what changes, and STOP makes it send the whole score.
- "Record the console", on the same tab, records what comes over the
  cable.


TIMEOUT WITH OBS

After "Set up OBS", during a timeout the camera picture shrinks to the
graphics window and comes back afterwards, in the same scene.
- Settings > Cameras shows the cameras in use. For camera 1,
  "Automatic" picks DroidCam, if there is one.
- "Adjust camera" puts it back to full screen.
- If OBS is on another computer, connect it by hand in Settings > OBS
  control > OBS connection set up by hand.
- With two to four cameras, Screens has a button per camera, and the one
  on air says "On air". You can change camera during a timeout: the
  picture stays in the window.
- In Settings > Cameras > "Camera during timeouts", choose the camera
  that goes on air for the timeout (the bench, for example). At the end the camera
  that was on air comes back. "None" keeps the camera on air.

If the tablet rotates or DroidCam changes resolution, the program
adjusts the camera again by itself. With "Full image", an upright
tablet has margins at the sides, and during a timeout those margins turn
blue.

If you already use a "Desconto" scene in OBS, you can keep it. Type its
name in Settings > OBS control > Old mode, and the program switches
scene as before.


TWO TO FOUR CAMERAS

The CAMERAS.txt guide, in this folder, explains how to connect two to
four cameras over Wi-Fi or on cable, and how much of the network each
one takes. DroidCam's free version stays at 480p; HD needs DroidCam Pro.
It also covers the camera for the Nothing screen, the "Show scoreboard"
switch and the inset of a second camera.


NEW VERSIONS

The first time, the panel asks whether you want to be told about new
versions. You can also change it in Settings > New versions. With it on,
Hallstream asks GitHub once a day and sends nothing about the club.

When a new version is out, the panel shows "Hallstream X is available"
(never while the stream is live). "Update now" downloads the new version,
checks it arrived whole, puts it in place of this one and restarts
Hallstream. OBS stays open and your data stays as it is. If anything
fails, you keep the version you had.

On Windows, Hallstream can only update itself in a folder it can write
to, such as C:\Hallstream; inside "Program Files" it can't. On a Mac,
only from the Applications folder.

The previous version stays beside it with ".old" at the end of its name
(Hallstream.exe.old, or Hallstream.app.old on a Mac), and is only deleted
once the new one has been open for 10 minutes. If the new one won't open,
delete it and take ".old" off the previous one's name.


BACKUP AND ANOTHER COMPUTER

In Settings > Backup, "Save backup" downloads a .zip file with the
"dados" folder: age groups, players, photos, sponsors, videos, settings
and the YouTube connection. It only works on the PC itself. Keep a copy
after you change anything, on a USB stick or in a folder just for the
club. Don't send it by email or WhatsApp: with it, anyone can stream on
the club's channel.

To move to another computer:
1. On the old computer, press "Save backup" and take the .zip on a USB
   stick.
2. On the new computer, install OBS 31 or newer and DroidCam. Unzip
   Hallstream to C:\Hallstream.
3. With Hallstream closed, unzip the backup: right-click the .zip,
   "Extract all", and type C:\Hallstream as the destination. Check that
   you end up with C:\Hallstream\dados, with everything you had.
4. Open Hallstream. In Settings, press "Set up OBS" and "Create desktop
   shortcut". If "Open with Windows" was on, it stays on.
5. Switch on the scoreboard console and wait for the box to say
   "Connected". If it won't connect, see "IF THE PROGRAM CAN'T FIND THE
   BOX".

If the old computer broke, use the last backup you saved. With no
backup, do the First-time setup again: the players and photos come back
from the FPB, but the sponsor logos and the videos have to be uploaded
again.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

- The box won't connect: check that the SL Live Stream app is closed on
  the phone and the tablet, and see the Scoring tab.
- OBS isn't "Ready", or the graphics don't appear: press "Repair" in
  the Screens tab, or "Set up OBS" in Settings.
- OBS won't connect: if it asks about safe mode, choose normal mode. In
  safe mode OBS turns off the WebSocket server.
- "Set up the YouTube account in OBS": in OBS, Settings > Stream,
  connect the YouTube account.
- A promo video has no sound: press "Set up OBS", which turns on the
  sound of the graphics source.
- The camera is crooked: Settings > OBS control > "Adjust camera".
- The panel won't open on the phone: use the address shown in Settings >
  Phone, with the phone on the same Wi-Fi network.
- A number looks wrong: send the file dados\raw.log.
- A warning that port 8765 is in use by another program: the scoreboard
  opened on another port. Press "Repair" or "Set up OBS", which changes
  the address in OBS, or close the other program and open Hallstream
  again.
- Any other problem: send the file dados\marcador.log, where the program
  writes what it has been doing.

The versions published on GitHub send nothing out of the PC. Only a
version built for a club, with its own key, sends the logs of each game
to that club's server at the end of the stream. Nothing personal goes
with them: no names, no passwords, no PIN.


LIVE STREAMS ON YOUTUBE

With access to the club's channel, Hallstream creates the public live
stream for each game by itself, with the title and the thumbnail, and at
the end makes the video unlisted. If the panel shows only the "Connect
to YouTube" button, just press it and sign in with the account that
manages the club's channel. If it asks for the ID and secret of an OAuth
client, the guide YouTube\CONNECT-YOUTUBE.txt has two parts.
In the first, on any computer and with any Google account, you create
the OAuth client in Google Cloud, with the app set to "In production".
In "Testing", the login expires every week.
In the second, on the club's PC, you paste the ID and the secret into
the First-time setup and sign in with the account that manages the
club's channel.
- The Streams tab lists the channel's live streams, those made by
  Hallstream and those made outside it, with filters by age group and by
  season. Each one has "Copy", "Open" and, if it is still public, "Make
  unlisted".
- A custom thumbnail only works with a verified channel
  (youtube.com/verify). Without that, the live stream still starts.
- If the panel says the YouTube connection has expired, go to Settings >
  YouTube and press "Connect to YouTube" again.
- The login is kept in the dados folder, in the file youtube.json. Don't
  send it to anyone. "Disconnect" revokes access on Google and deletes
  it.
