# 016: In-app updates (a dialog that downloads, swaps and restarts)

Status: all five layers written, in review as one stack. The real swap gets its first proof when an installed release is offered the next one.

Repo `app/` at `849a394` (main, v0.1.5). The owner asked on 2026-11-27: "I'd love this to automatically update, doesn't bva do that already? Maybe like a pop up suggesting to update?", then to ship it unattended. Each 🔥 row is a pick the owner can overturn before its PR merges.

## BVA, and why Hallstream differs

BVA is Electron and uses `update-electron-app` (Squirrel through update.electronjs.org; it replaced `electron-updater`), on Windows and macOS only (`basketball-video-analyzer/app/src/main/autoUpdater.ts:2-17`): it checks at start and every 6 h with no opt-in, downloads the new version in the background, then asks to restart. Hallstream keeps the popup and the one-click install, and changes two things for game day (decisions 1 and 4): the check stays a choice, because the privacy page promises nothing is sent without one; and nothing downloads until the click, because a 30 to 50 MB background download on hall wifi can starve a live stream.

## Facts found

- `internal/update/update.go` asks `https://api.github.com/repos/kauredo/hallstream/releases/latest` and returns `Release{Version, URL}` (json `version`, `url`); `Newer` ignores builds that are not plain X.Y.Z, so dev builds never update.
- `internal/web/updates.go`: `RunUpdates` checks at start, every 24 h and when the switch turns on, only while `settings.update_check` is on (off by default, `internal/store/store.go:167`). `newerRelease()` is returned as `"update"` in `GET /api/status` (`internal/web/server.go:518`); the `Release` struct is serialised as it is.
- Panel: the `#updateAsk` box (`web/painel/index.html:42`; `laterVersion`/`renderUpdateAsk` at `web/painel/painel.js:3524-3531`) shows the version with a link to the release page and "Agora não", remembered per version in `localStorage` `hs-update-later`. Definições > Versões novas has the switch (`painel.js:2265`); its hint `painel.atualizacao.avisar_dica` says it "downloads nothing", and `painel.atualizacao.dica` tells people to download from the release page.
- The desktop-shortcut question (`#shortcutAsk`, `index.html:47`, `renderShortcutAsk` at `painel.js:3542`) only shows when `status.shortcut` is true, which is Windows only (`desktop.Shortcut` is nil on Mac and Linux). Its setting `desktop_shortcut` is kept out of `PUT /api/settings` (`server.go:564`).
- Release files (`build.sh:55,103,119`, `.github/workflows/release.yml:354`): `Hallstream-X.zip` (`Hallstream.exe`, `Hallstream (32-bit).exe` and texts), `Hallstream-X-macos.dmg` (signed, notarised, stapled `Hallstream.app` + `Applications` link), `Hallstream-X-linux-x64.tar.gz` (`Hallstream/Hallstream`), and `SHA256SUMS` (`<hex>  <file>` lines). `release.yml:255` already mounts the DMG with `hdiutil attach -nobrowse -readonly`.
- Data: `dados/` beside the exe on Windows and Linux; `~/Library/Application Support/Hallstream/dados` for the Mac app (`store.go:452-460`). Swapping the program never touches it.
- A second start only opens the first copy's panel and exits (the `web.Ours` loop at `cmd/hallstream/main.go:140`). `web.Listen` takes the first free port from 8765 to 8775 (`server.go:184`).
- `desktop.Tray.Run` blocks until "Sair"; `Quit` (end of `main.go`) stops the camera, calls `srv.CloseOBS()`, closes Bluetooth, the store and the log. `CloseOBS` stops a live stream when `close_on_exit` is on (`internal/web/obs.go:819`). Besides "Sair", SIGINT/SIGTERM (`watchSignals`) and the Dock's Quit (`onQuit`) end it; all paths go through the `sync.Once` around `Quit` in `Tray.Run`, so a new `Stop` channel can't run the clean-up twice.
- Detached starts already exist: `internal/obsapp/obsapp_linux.go:56-61` (`Setsid`, `go cmd.Wait()`), `internal/desktop/desktop_windows.go:89` (`SysProcAttr`). `internal/fpb/fpb.go:92-97` caps redirects with a host check. `archive/zip` is used in `internal/web/app.go`.
- `Server.Streaming()` (`obs.go:804`) asks OBS now, falling back to the cached `s.stream` (kept every 3 s). `isLoopback(r.RemoteAddr)` marks requests from the PC itself; `Server.once` runs one-off background work.
- Patterns to reuse: `io.LimitReader` caps as in `internal/web/calendar.go:89`; temp-then-rename as in `store.WriteFile` (`store.go:542`).

## Decisions

| # | Rating | Question | Pick | Why / risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🔥 | Who sees the offer, given `update_check` is off by default? | Keep the switch and its default. Ask once on the PC itself, on every system, in a new box `#checkAsk` beside `#updateAsk`: "Quer que o Hallstream avise quando houver uma versão nova?" with **Sim** and **Não**. Both call `POST /api/update/ask` `{ "check": bool }` (loopback only, 403 otherwise), which sets `update_asked` (bool) and `update_check`. `putSettings` keeps the saved `update_asked`, as it keeps `desktop_shortcut` (`server.go:564`), so a stale tab or a phone can't bring the question back. Status `"update_ask": true` while loopback, not asked and the check is off. `#checkAsk` shows before `#shortcutAsk`; only one question shows at a time. | The privacy page promises nothing is sent without a choice, and the saved settings can't tell "never chose" from "chose off". Risk: people click Não and never update; Definições still has the switch. |
| 2 | ✅ | What the offer looks like | `#updateAsk` becomes "Hallstream X está disponível" with **Atualizar agora**, **Mais tarde** (today's per-version memory) and an "O que mudou" link. Where this copy can't install itself (decision 7), the box keeps today's "Ver a versão" link instead of the button. | Reuses the box people know. |
| 3 | ✅ | Never mid-game | The panel hides the box while `S.status.obs.stream` is active (status already carries it, `obs.go:767`). `POST /api/update` answers 409 while `Streaming()`, checks `Streaming()` again just before the swap and again just before the restart, and gives up (undoing the swap) if the stream went live. An update restart never runs `CloseOBS`: OBS stays open, whatever `close_on_exit` says. Only from the PC itself (403 otherwise). | The update must never stop a broadcast, and closing OBS on an update would surprise everyone. |
| 4 | 🔥 | Download | No background download (see the BVA note). On the click: the release JSON gives each asset's `browser_download_url`; only URLs starting `https://github.com/kauredo/hallstream/releases/download/` are kept; redirects are followed at most 5 times and only to `github.com` or a `*.githubusercontent.com` host (`CheckRedirect`, as in `fpb.go`); size cap `var maxDownload int64 = 300 << 20` (a var so tests lower it); 10 min through the context. `SHA256SUMS` from the same release must list the file and match. Mac: the new app must pass `codesign --verify --deep --strict` and its `TeamIdentifier` (from `codesign -dv --verbose=2`, stderr) must equal the running app's. | The checksum arrives over the same HTTPS, so it catches corruption more than attacks; HTTPS to GitHub is the trust, plus the Developer ID on a Mac. Windows builds are unsigned, so nothing stronger exists there yet. |
| 5 | ✅ | Swap | Stage in `<parent of target>/.hallstream-update/` (same volume), then remove any stale `<target>.old`, rename `<target>` → `<target>.old` and staged → `<target>`; any failure before the new copy starts renames back. Target: Windows and Linux, `os.Executable()` resolved; Mac, the bundle three levels up from `Contents/MacOS/hallstream` (must end in `.app`), staged with `ditto` from the mounted DMG. The new copy deletes `<target>.old` and `.hallstream-update/` 10 minutes after it starts listening, so a copy that dies at once leaves the old one to rename back. | Windows can rename a running exe but not overwrite it; the same rule works everywhere. |
| 6 | ✅ | Restart | `web.Server.Restart func() error` (set by main) starts the new copy with the current arguments, minus any old `-restarted`, plus `-nobrowser -restarted`: the target exe directly on Windows (`SysProcAttr` as in `desktop_windows.go:89`) and Linux (`Setsid` as in `obsapp_linux.go:56-61`), `open -n -a <bundle> --args …` on a Mac; per-system files `cmd/hallstream/restart_windows.go`, `restart_darwin.go`, `restart_linux.go`, `restart_other.go` (returns an error). If starting fails it returns the error and the update undoes the swap. If it starts, main sets `restarting` (an `atomic.Bool`), closes `Tray.Stop`, and the tray quits with the normal clean-up minus `CloseOBS`. `-restarted` (bool) makes the new copy poll every 250 ms, up to 30 s, until no port from 8765 to 8775 answers `web.Ours`, then carry on (after 30 s it carries on anyway and the usual "already open" check decides). | Same clean-up as "Sair". The old copy frees its port, so the new one normally takes the same port and the panel's reload lands. |
| 7 | 🔥 | Where updating refuses | Worked out once, at start (the folder probe and `codesign -dv` are too slow for every status poll), and kept in the server; the route checks again before downloading. Status `"update"` carries `"install": false` (link only) when: not loopback; `GOOS`/`GOARCH` has no asset; the target's folder isn't writable (checked by creating and removing a temp file, e.g. unzipped into Program Files); on a Mac, not inside a `.app`, the path contains `/AppTranslocation/` or starts with `/Volumes/`, or the running app has no `TeamIdentifier` (ad-hoc builds). | Each refusal keeps the release-page link working. |
| 8 | ✅ | Request model | `POST /api/update` starts one background job through `Server.once` (409 if one runs) and answers 202. The job defers, in this order: undo of the swap unless the restart started, state `"failed"` unless it reached `"restarting"`, clearing the busy flag; so a panic never leaves a missing program or a stuck "downloading". Status `"update"` gains `"state"`: `""`, `"downloading"`, `"installing"`, `"restarting"`, `"failed"`, and `"error"` (the reason, in the panel's language) when failed. The panel polls `/api/status` as it already does; at `"restarting"` it shows "A reiniciar…", polls every 2 s (a failed fetch counts as still restarting) and reloads when `version` changes, or after 60 s says "Abra o Hallstream outra vez". | A 10-minute request would die with the tab. |
| 9 | ✅ | Data shape and test seams | `update.Release` gets `Assets map[string]string \`json:"-"\``. `web.Server` gets `Updater` (an interface with `Target() (string, error)`, `Download`, `Prepare`, `Swap`), defaulting to the real `internal/update` functions, so route tests never rename the test binary; tests put a `Release` straight into `s.newer`. Windows keeps the other exe and the texts from the old zip: only the running exe is replaced, on purpose. `newerRelease()` is replaced by `updateStatus(loopback bool) map[string]any` returning `version`, `url`, `install`, `state`, `error`, or nil. | Asset URLs stay out of the status. |
| 10 | ✅ | Words and contract | pt.json first, then en.json: the box, the ask-once question, the states and refusals, `app.flag.after`, log lines for download, check, swap, restart, undo and clean-up. `avisar_dica` becomes "Só descarrega quando carregar em Atualizar agora."; `painel.atualizacao.dica` rewritten; `docs/CONTRACT.md` "Versões novas" (line 400) rewritten; comments at `store.go:166` and `updates.go:17` updated. | Repo rules. |
| 11 | ✅ | Stack | 1 plan · 2 download and check · 3 install and restart · 4 panel, route, ask-once, words, contract · 5 docs. | Pure code first; the risky swap is one layer reviewers can read alone. |

## Layers

### 1. `01-docs/plan-016`: this file, plans/README row.

### 2. `02-feat/update-download` (`internal/update`)
- `Release.Assets` (decision 9), filled from the release JSON's `assets[].name`/`browser_download_url`, dropping URLs outside the prefix.
- `AssetName(goos, goarch, version string) string`: `Hallstream-X.zip` for windows amd64 and 386, `Hallstream-X-macos.dmg` for darwin, `Hallstream-X-linux-x64.tar.gz` for linux amd64, `""` otherwise.
- `Download(ctx context.Context, c *http.Client, rel Release, name, dir string) (string, error)`: fetches `SHA256SUMS` and the asset (both must be in `Assets`), streams the asset through `io.LimitReader` and sha256 into `dir/name`, compares, removes the file on any failure.
- Tests with `httptest` (point `Assets` at the test server by building `Release` in the test): good file; wrong hash; file missing from sums; oversize; redirect followed; `Latest` drops an asset outside the prefix; `AssetName` table.

### 3. `03-feat/update-install`
- `internal/update`: `Target() (string, error)` (decision 5), `Installable(target string) error` (decision 7's folder and Mac checks, returning an `i18n.Err` with the reason), `Prepare(archive, target string) (staged string, err error)` per system (`install_windows.go`, `install_darwin.go`, `install_linux.go`, `install_other.go`): pick `Hallstream (32-bit).exe` when `GOARCH=386` else `Hallstream.exe` from the zip / `hdiutil attach -nobrowse -readonly -mountpoint <tmp>`, `ditto <mount>/Hallstream.app <staged>`, `hdiutil detach`, `codesign` checks / `Hallstream/Hallstream` from the tar, mode 0755. `Swap(staged, target string) (undo func() error, err error)` shared by all systems. `Cleanup(target string)`.
- macOS commands run through `var run = func(name string, args ...string) ([]byte, error)` so tests swap in a fake.
- `internal/desktop`: `Tray.Stop <-chan struct{}`; `tray.go` and `desktop_other.go` quit on it like "Sair" without asking.
- `cmd/hallstream`: `-restarted` flag and wait (decision 6); the `restart_*.go` files; `restarting` (`atomic.Bool`) so `Quit` skips `CloseOBS`; `srv.Restart`; the 10-minute delayed `update.Cleanup` started through `safely`-style one-off once the server listens.
- Tests: `Swap` and undo in a temp dir; zip pick by arch; tar pick; `Installable` refuses a read-only folder and translocated/`/Volumes` paths; darwin `Prepare` with the fake `run` (wrong Team ID refused); `-restarted` wait returns once a fake listener closes.

## Done when

On a supported system with `update_check` on and a newer release published, the panel offers "Hallstream X está disponível" off air only; Atualizar agora downloads the right file, checks it against `SHA256SUMS` (and the Team ID on a Mac), swaps the program and restarts it on the same panel without closing OBS; the `.old` copy is removed 10 minutes later; a failure before the restart, or the stream going live, leaves the running version in place and says why; `dados/` is untouched; every new install is asked once, on the PC, whether to check for versions.

## Not tested before merge

The real swap needs two published releases; it gets its proof when v0.1.7 is offered to an installed v0.1.6. A new copy that starts and then dies needs the `.old` renamed back by hand (the readme says how). Windows antivirus reaction to a self-replacing unsigned exe is unknown until then.

## Plan review

Two goldfish rounds. Round 1 (revise) led to the background job, the live re-check before the swap and the restart, no `CloseOBS` on an update restart, and the ask-once box on every system. Round 2's one blocker was a wrong fact about BVA's updater, checked against `basketball-video-analyzer/app/src/main/autoUpdater.ts:2-17` and fixed; its majors pinned `POST /api/update/ask`, the `Updater` seam, the deferred undo, and computing `install` once at start.
